# Timing interpretation

`runtime.csv` records preprocessing (timestamp source verification/recovery and feature construction), matched dwell mining, candidate feature checking, greedy selection, classifier training, and inference as separately labelled phases. `selection_cost.csv` additionally reports initial/eligible/selected candidates, completed candidate-refit counts and measured selection-step wall time. Candidate classifier fits are included in selection; the winning fitted classifier is retained without an extra final refit. They must not be counted twice.

M0/M1 and the Act+Rel pool are reused historical artifacts. Their original costs were paid previously, not zero-cost training; the current runtime does not remeasure them. Historical mining timing/provenance accompanies preparation.json. M2 baseline classifier training is measured separately. Neural training starts from cached inputs and includes validation/checkpoint epochs; it excludes timestamp recovery, mining and LR feature selection. Report parameter counts and epochs from per-run config/freeze/epochs files.

Inference timings begin with loaded R/S/T and include encoding/scaling, selected-logic checking and prediction. Neural inference additionally includes model loading in this implementation. They exclude raw source extraction, upstream R/S/T construction and disk reads performed before the timer. These boundaries are deliberately explicit and are not an end-to-end speed claim or a controlled hardware benchmark. Initial mining/selection processes run concurrently on the same machine, so wall times reflect contention. Resume/aborted attempts and log records must be retained; completed selection-step times do not recover work lost before a step checkpoint. Do not sum overlapping invocation and step timings.
